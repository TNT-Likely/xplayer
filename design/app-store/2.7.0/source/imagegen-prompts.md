## 标题

Use case: ads-marketing. Design a wordless cinematic product-page masthead for XPlayer, an independent live-TV playlist player with a dark pine-green interface and pale sage accents. An original hero photograph/physical sculpture titled “Tidal Signal.” In a low-lit emerald sea, at the exact centre a compact freestanding horizontal widescreen made of subtly beveled thick optical glass reveals a spectacular luminous jade breaking ocean wave, like a moving image made physical. The wave in the glass is a vivid, sculptural crest with warm ivory foam; a hint of spray escapes just at one edge. Two very thin, barely offset sheet-glass echoes trailing behind the main rectangle suggest switching channels. The image inside the glass has noticeably greater saturation and light than the water and sky around it. Gently oblique three-quarter perspective, primarily front-on. No console or monitor hardware. Reflections and two long, elegant ribbons of backlit current extend horizontally far into both edges; sea haze, glints of warm pearl on the wave, tactile optics. Think an art-directed long-exposure album cover, visually distinctive at thumbnail scale, with a strong silhouette even in an aggressive wide crop. Overall dark forest, sea glass, faded gold and pale mint, no purple, no cyberpunk interface. Compose as an extremely WIDE panorama for final aspect 21:9, full bleed. The WHOLE central freestanding screen, the two panes, and the contained crest together occupy only 33% of total canvas width and 32% of total canvas height, dead centred; almost all of the brightest details must lie within this small central object. Keep top and bottom quiet to survive cropping. Richly textured continuous water runs edge to edge; no distant subjects or human figures. No lettering, text, digits, watermarks, logos, play icon, interface, speakers, stand, poster border, cards, additional panels or external screens.

Cinematographer's camera at the surface of the ocean at blue hour. One incredible, physically realistic translucent emerald breaking wave, a single luminous curling barrel with fine wind-blown ivory foam and a pale golden rim, concentrated on the FAR RIGHT, crest centred at x76%, y43%. Close, visceral water texture, warm film grain, lit from the far right, a little warm dawn haze on the far right of the horizon. The sea runs across the whole frame, deep pine-green to black-teal on the left with atmospheric mist and softly graded low contrast, slight worn jade light on the ripples across the bottom.
